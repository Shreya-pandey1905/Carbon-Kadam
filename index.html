Problem Statement

Climate change is driven by greenhouse gases, but most students and families never see how their daily choices (electricity, travel, food, waste) add up. Many carbon calculators are foreign-centric, long and boring.

Our answer

CarbonKadam is a lightweight, India-focused web tool. Enter a few daily habits, get your yearly CO₂ footprint in under a minute, and see simple, personal steps to cut it.

Who is it for?

  • College students and hostel residents
  • Families who want to lower bills and emissions
  • Schools and clubs running climate awareness drives

SDG 13: Climate Action

Goal 13 of the UN Sustainable Development Goals calls for urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts.

TargetMeaning
13.1Strengthen resilience to climate-related hazards and disasters
13.2Integrate climate measures into policies and planning
13.3Improve education, awareness and capacity on climate action

CarbonKadam mainly supports Target 13.3 by making climate education personal and interactive.

Objectives

  1. Help users estimate their yearly carbon footprint with simple inputs.
  2. Show which habit contributes the most emissions.
  3. Compare the result with Indian and world averages.
  4. Give practical, low-cost steps to reduce emissions.
  5. Build a habit through a quiz and a personal pledge.

How It Works

Total yearly footprint = Electricity + LPG + Travel + Food + Waste.

InputFactor used (approx.)
Electricity0.73 kg CO₂ per kWh (CEA grid average, 2023-24), divided by people in the home
LPGAbout 42 kg CO₂ per 14.2 kg cylinder, divided by people in the home
Travel0 to 0.17 kg CO₂ per km, depending on mode
Food1.7, 2.5 or 3.3 kg CO₂e per day by diet type
Waste100, 250 or 500 kg CO₂e per year (simple assumption)

Technology

Built with only HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It runs in any browser, works offline and stores no personal data.

Flow

  1. User enters habits
  2. JavaScript multiplies each habit by its factor
  3. Results are shown as a chart with comparisons
  4. The biggest source gets a personal tip
